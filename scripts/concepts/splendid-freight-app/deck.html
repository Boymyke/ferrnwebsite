What we noticed

Ten years of strong service. A website that hides it.

Splendid Freight has the hard part: offices in Abuja, Lagos and Port Harcourt, every mode of transport and a decade of clients. Online, very little of that reaches the people searching for a freight partner.

From a quick outside review of splendidfreight.com. We want to confirm each point with you today.

  • Google sees an almost empty pageThe site is built so content loads only after scripts run, with default page titles. Search engines and link previews get very little to work with.
  • No pages for the industries you serveBuyers search for pharma logistics, FMCG distribution or project cargo. Dedicated pages win those searches.
  • Quotes start with a phone callA structured quote request captures origin, destination, cargo and weight on the first contact, any time of day.
  • No view of what the website brings inVisitors, enquiries and which pages win business are hard to see, so the site cannot be improved with confidence.

Today's conversation

What we would love to learn from you.

01Your business

  • Which services and routes bring in most of your revenue?
  • Who are your typical clients, and which industries matter most?
  • What would you like to be known for in five years?

02How quotes work today

  • How do new enquiries reach you, and who answers them?
  • How long does it take to send a quote?
  • Roughly how many enquiries a month, and how many become jobs?

03Tracking and updates

  • How do clients get shipment updates now?
  • Which systems hold your shipment data: software, spreadsheets, carrier portals?
  • Which questions do clients call about most?

04Documents and payments

  • Which documents do you collect most often, and where do delays happen?
  • How do clients receive invoices and pay today?
  • Do you work in naira, dollars or both?

05Your team

  • Who would use a staff dashboard, and across which offices?
  • Who will own the website and portal on your side?
  • What tools does the team already use every day?

06Goals and timing

  • What would make this project a clear success for you?
  • Is there a date that matters, like a launch, tender or season?
  • Which phase would you like to start with?
