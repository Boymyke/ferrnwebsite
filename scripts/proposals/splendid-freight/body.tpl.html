01 · What we understood

Moving cargo is the work. The hard part is keeping customers informed.

Today, a shipment lives in Outlook, Zoho Books and phone calls. Customers chase updates, and delays are found late.

The goal

One record for every shipment and ATM job, shared by your team and your customers.
