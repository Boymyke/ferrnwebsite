01 · What we heard

Moving cargo is the work. The hard part is keeping customers informed.

Today a shipment lives across Outlook, Zoho Books and phone calls. Customers chase updates, and it is hard to see which stage slows things down.

The goal

One record for every shipment and every ATM job, that your team and your customers both see.

QuotedWhat did the customer approve?
TrackedWhere is the cargo right now?
FlaggedWhat is late, and who knows?

04ATM jobs off the record

02 · How it works

Update once. Everyone sees it.

Every shipment and every ATM gets a QR label. Staff scan it at each handover and pick the step. The customer's tracking, notifications and reports update on their own.
