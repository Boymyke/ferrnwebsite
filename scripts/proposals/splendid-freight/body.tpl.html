01 · Understanding of Problem

Moving cargo is the work. The hard part is keeping customers informed.

Today a shipment lives across emails, Zoho Books and phone calls. Customers ask where their cargo is, staff answer one by one, and nobody can say how long a Manchester to Lagos air shipment takes on average, or which stage causes the most delays.

Core understanding

Splendid Freight does not need another tracking link. It needs one operating record for every shipment and every ATM job.

Quote, approval, invoice, pickup, airport or port, customs, inland trucking and delivery should all write to the same timeline, so the customer, the operations team and management see the same truth.

01Status by email

Customers chase updates over email and phone because there is no account where they can see milestones for themselves.

02Manual quote cycle

Requests arrive as emails with weights, dimensions and addresses, then move by hand into quotes, approvals and Zoho Books invoices.

03Delays found late

Customs holds and missed connections are discovered when a customer calls, not when the milestone is overdue.

04ATM jobs off the system

Decommissioning, relocation, staging, civil works, cannibalisation and refurbishment need their own trail of machines, parts and sign-offs.

02 · Approach & Methodology

Scan at every handover. Let the system tell the customer.

Every shipment and every ATM gets a QR code. Staff scan it at pickup, at the airport or port, at customs and at delivery. Each scan updates the customer's account, sends the right notification and feeds the analytics, so updates happen without anyone writing an email.

03 · Scope & Timeline

A full platform in 16 weeks. Live by mid March 2027.

Delivery runs from kickoff through specification, design, build, testing with your team and launch. The website and customer portal go first, the QR tracking and ATM module follow, and every date moves with the kickoff date.

Customer accounts

Each B2B customer gets its own login with multiple users, shipment history, documents, invoices and saved addresses. B2C ready for the next phase.

Quote portal

Customers request quotes with origin, destination, weight, dimensions, commodity and pickup address. Staff price and send the quote, the customer approves online.

Shipment tracking

Milestones from pickup to origin airport or port, departure, arrival, customs in progress, customs cleared, inland trucking and delivery. Port to port supported.

QR scanning

Printable QR labels for every shipment and ATM. Staff scan from any phone to confirm milestones with time, location and photo proof.

Delay alerts

Expected time for each stage. When a milestone is overdue the team is alerted and the customer gets a clear update by email and WhatsApp.

Analytics & customer stats

Average days per shipment and per stage, on-time rate, customs time, lane and customer performance, quote conversion and revenue views.

ATM support module

Decommissioning, relocation from branch A to branch B, staging, civil works, cannibalisation and refurbishment, with a QR tagged record per machine.

Zoho Books, Outlook & insurance

Approved quotes become Zoho Books invoices, emails go out through your Outlook domain, and cargo insurance cover is offered and recorded in the quote.

Website rebuild

A faster, SEO ready website with service pages for freight, warehousing and ATM logistics, quote requests, a blog, visitor analytics and an AI assistant.

Roles, security & audit

Admin, operations, field staff and customer roles. Every quote, scan and status change is logged with who did it and when.
