# Content workflow (X, Instagram, TikTok)

## Where things live

- **Studio → Content → Calendar.** Every post with its platform, time (Lagos), copy, hashtags and media.
  - **Import November calendar** loads the 30 planned posts in one click. Running it again skips posts that are already there.
- **Studio → Content → Generator.** Drafts new posts in Ferrn's voice from a topic, platform and goal. Treat these as first drafts: they always need a human edit.
- **Studio → Carousel studio.** Paste "Slide N: …" lines from a post and download branded 1080×1350 PNG slides.
- **Studio → Content → Performance.** After posting, add the numbers to each post: impressions, likes, comments, shares, saves, clicks, leads. This page then shows what's working by platform, format and theme.

## Weekly rhythm

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Friday | Review next week in the calendar. Approve copy, and make carousels in Carousel studio. |
| Saturday | Film the week's talking-head and screen-recording pieces in one sitting (about 45 minutes). |
| Daily | Post at the scheduled times: X 9:00, Instagram 12:00, TikTok 18:00 (Lagos). Use **Copy caption**, then **Open platform**. |
| Monday | Fill in last week's numbers and check Performance. Do more of what drove DMs and leads, not just likes. |

## Quality rules (no "AI slop")

- Every post makes one specific point a business owner can act on. No generic "In today's digital landscape…".
- People on camera are real Ferrn people. Don't use AI-generated humans to stand in for staff or clients.
- **AI video** (Google Flow / Veo, Seedance): only for abstract b-roll such as a phone lighting up with a WhatsApp reminder, never for fake testimonials or fake screens.
- **ElevenLabs:** only for voice-over on screen recordings, using a voice you have rights to. Say so in the caption if asked.
- **Stock images:** only as background texture, never as "our team" or "our client".
- Testimonials: real clients only, with written permission, and quoted exactly.
- Reviews (Trustpilot, Google): ask everyone, offer no incentives, and use neutral wording.

## Scheduling

Posting is manual for now: Studio holds the queue and you post from the platforms. When you're ready to schedule automatically, sign in to Meta Business Suite (Instagram), X and TikTok in the browser and Claude can schedule the week's posts from the calendar for you.
